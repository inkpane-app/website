Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Inkpane works on your Mac. Your captures, recordings, the text recognised in them, and the rest of your library stay on your Mac, and we never receive them. This policy explains the few cases where data does leave your Mac, and why.

It covers the Inkpane app for macOS (“the app”) and the website inkpane.app. Both are made by the independent developer of Inkpane (“Inkpane”, “we”, “us”). Questions and requests: support@inkpane.app.

In short

  • No account, no cloud library, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. We don’t sell data.
  • Captures, recordings, OCR text and the library database never leave your Mac unless you send them somewhere yourself, or an AI agent or AI provider you set up receives them.
  • The app contacts our download server to check for updates, and Lemon Squeezy to check a license once you have one. Nothing else is sent to us.

What stays on your Mac

All of the following is stored only on your Mac, in your user folder. We have no copy of any of it.

  • Captures and recordings: screenshots, screen recordings with optional microphone sound, GIFs, pins, and annotation files. By default these are in ~/Library/Application Support/app.inkpane.Inkpane/, or in the folder you choose.
  • The library: a database of your captures. For each capture it holds the file location, size, date, the app and window it came from, the browser address (for Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave and Edge), the Git project, branch and remote (with credentials removed), tags, notes, and the text recognised in the image. Text recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework.
  • Settings, logs and diagnostics: your preferences, the app’s own log, crash and performance reports from macOS, the list of AI agents you allowed, and a log of what they asked for (the newest 500 entries).
  • Keychain items: your license record and the API keys you enter for AI services, in the macOS Keychain on this Mac only. The license record includes a one-way hash of your Mac’s hardware ID. It is used to notice a license copied to another Mac, and it is never sent anywhere.

Unpinned captures move to the macOS Trash after the period you choose (7 days, 30 days, or never). To remove everything, quit the app and delete the folder above, the app, and the Keychain items named app.inkpane.Inkpane.license and app.inkpane.Inkpane.ai.

When data leaves your Mac

Update checks

The app asks downloads.inkpane.app whether a new version is available. It checks automatically if you allow it (Settings → General), or when you choose Check for Updates. During the free Pro period, the app also reads the current date from the same server, so the end of the offer doesn’t depend on your Mac’s clock alone.

These requests carry your IP address and the app’s name and version, as any web request does. The server is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep short-lived logs under its own privacy policy. We look only at totals, such as how many update checks or downloads there were. We don’t build profiles. No system information is sent.

Licenses

Once licenses are on sale, purchases go through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles your payment, invoice, email address and taxes under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details we need to support you, such as your email address, the product and the license key, but never your card details.

When you activate a key, the app sends the key and your Mac’s name (as set in System Settings → General → Sharing) to Lemon Squeezy. It then checks the license about once a day, and frees the activation when you deactivate this Mac.

AI features you turn on

AI quick actions, and translation with an AI service or DeepL, are off until you add your own API key. When you use one, the app sends the request directly from your Mac to the provider you chose: Anthropic, OpenAI or another OpenAI-compatible service, or DeepL. It never goes through us.

A request can include the image (unless you turn images off), the text recognised in it, the app and window it came from, the page address, and your prompt. If you turn them on in Settings → Privacy, email addresses and phone numbers are masked first. The provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives.

Apple Intelligence models and Apple Translation run on your Mac. Local servers such as Ollama or LM Studio stay on your Mac too.

AI agents you connect

Inkpane includes a local server (MCP) that AI agents on your Mac, such as Claude Code or Codex, can use. It accepts connections only from your own user account on this Mac, through a protected local socket, or through 127.0.0.1 with a secret token.

Each agent must be allowed before it can see anything. The first time it asks, you choose Allow Once, Always Allow or Deny, and you can revoke it later in Settings → Privacy. While an agent is working, an indicator shows at the top of the screen, and every request is written to the local log.

An agent you allow can take screenshots, read the text and the accessibility structure of windows, list windows, read recent library items (including their app, title, address and Git project), record the screen, and draw on it. Apps on the exclusion list are hidden from agents: by default that includes password managers, banking and payment apps, and messaging apps. Optional masking of emails, phone numbers and password fields also applies to what agents receive.

What an agent receives may be sent on to that agent’s own AI provider, under that agent’s terms. Inkpane can’t control that, so allow only agents you trust.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and help you, and we keep them only as long as that requires.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Screen Recording (required): to take captures and recordings, and to capture for agents you allow.
  • Accessibility (optional): to find password fields to mask, to read the accessibility structure of a window for an agent, to recognise the project open in your editor, and to show the keys you press in a recording. Keystrokes appear only in that recording’s picture and are not logged.
  • Microphone (optional): to record your voice with a screen recording.
  • Automation of browsers (asked per browser): to read the address of the front tab when you capture it.

The app doesn’t use your camera or location.

The website

inkpane.app is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Your choice of light or dark mode is kept in your browser’s local storage and never sent. GitHub and, for downloads, Cloudflare may log technical data such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Sharing

We don’t sell, rent or share personal information. Data reaches others only as described above: the AI providers and agents you choose, Lemon Squeezy for purchases, and our hosting providers.

Your rights

Most of your data is on your Mac and under your control, so you can view, export or delete it at any time. For anything we or our providers hold about you, such as purchase records or emails, write to support@inkpane.app. You can ask to access it, correct it or delete it, and we’ll answer within 30 days.

These rights come from the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and, where they apply to you, laws such as the GDPR. You can also complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner or your local data protection authority.

Inkpane isn’t directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your Mac, we’ll also say so in the app’s release notes.

This policy is also available in other languages. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version prevails, unless the law where you live says otherwise.
