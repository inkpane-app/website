How it works

Stop describing pixels.

One gesture replaces a screenshot, a file path and a paragraph of explanation.

  1. 01

    You circle it.

    Hold a hotkey and draw over the broken button. Nothing to save, nothing to explain.

  2. 02

    Your agent sees it.

    read_screen_ocr
  3. 03

    It points back.

    The fix lands in your code, and a box lands on your screen where it changed.

iPhone & iPad

Your iPhone and iPad, too.

Inkpane draws on whatever your Mac shows, including the screen of an iPhone or iPad. Mark up an app demo, record a walkthrough with live ink, or circle a bug on a real device for your agent.

Sidecar

Use your iPad as a second display. Inkpane treats it like any other screen: draw, spotlight and record on it.

iPhone Mirroring

Your iPhone opens in a window on your Mac. Stick marks to that window and they move with it.

Cable + QuickTime

Plug in an iPhone or iPad and pick it as the screen in a QuickTime movie recording. Then record its screen with your ink on top.

iOS Simulator

Annotate and record the Simulator while you build. It’s handy for app demos, reviews and bug reports.

There’s nothing to install on the iPhone or iPad. Inkpane runs on your Mac, where their screens show up.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your screen is the most personal thing on your Mac. Inkpane never sends it anywhere you didn’t choose.

On your Mac.

Capture, OCR, search and Apple Translation run on-device. No account, no cloud: your captures never reach us.

You approve every agent.

Each MCP client asks once, and a pill at the top of the screen shows whenever one is looking.

Sensitive apps stay out.

Agents can’t see password managers, banking or chat apps, by default. Emails and phone numbers can be masked automatically.

Your key, your provider.

AI actions use your own API key, kept in your Keychain. No resold tokens.

Questions.

Which agents does it work with?

Claude Code and Codex, set up in one click from Settings → Agents. Anything that speaks MCP — Cursor, Claude Desktop and others — can use the same server.

Does my screen get uploaded anywhere?

No. Inkpane runs entirely on your Mac. A capture only goes to an agent you approved, when you or it asks, and to an AI provider only when you run a quick action.

What do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Some extras, like Apple Translation, need macOS 15.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

No, and you don’t need one. Inkpane runs on your Mac and draws on iPhone and iPad screens shown there: through Sidecar, iPhone Mirroring, a cable and QuickTime, or the iOS Simulator.

How is it different from CleanShot X?

Inkpane covers the capture basics, but it is built around your agent: the agent sees what you circle and draws back on your screen.

How do updates work?

Inkpane checks for updates on its own and asks before installing one.
